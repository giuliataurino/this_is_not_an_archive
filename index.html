Moonlight, Strandgade 30

Vilhelm Hammershøi

Danish

1900–1906

Hammershøi depicted this parlor in his apartment in Copenhagen at morning, noon, and night. In this scene, he employed deep lavender hues and velvety brushwork to capture the effect of moonlight. The emptiness of the space accentuates the qualities of the illumination and the geometric patterns of the architecture.
